About these numbers

  • Real benchmark, not a live factory. The jobs and machine energy profiles come from a published production line (3 machines, 6 days).
  • Sample tariff. Prices are Pakistani industrial rates as reported in news (Rs35.68 peak, Rs22.83 off-peak). The 18:30–22:30 peak window is our assumption.
  • Low-energy settings. Most of the saving comes from running a step in its lower-energy setting. We assume it makes the same product; this is not yet confirmed with the data authors.
  • "Before" is our own baseline: a simple schedule that always picks the fastest setting and ignores prices.
