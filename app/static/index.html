Benchmark methodology & assumptions
Navitas is demonstrated on a public GECAD textile hang-tag production dataset with three machines over six operating days. The optimiser produces a schedule; an independent checker evaluates dataset rules and user-added constraints. The Pakistani tariff scenario uses illustrative peak/off-peak rates and an assumed peak window; these inputs are not a substitute for a factory's current electricity tariff. Lower-energy machine modes are treated as equivalent in production quality, but that equivalence has not been confirmed by the dataset authors. Results are modelled benchmark outputs, not measured savings from a commercial factory deployment. Navitas is decision-support software and does not directly control factory machinery.
